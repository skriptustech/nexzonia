Debug Dungeon PROTOTYPE · throwaway

Nexzonia · a service is on fire and you are the on-call engineer.

The question this prototype answers: does the core debugging loop feel like real incident work, and is it fun?

The loop: you have limited Focus to investigate, while customers are being lost every action (Impact). You can look (logs, metrics, what changed, traces, isolation), mitigate to slow the bleeding, then form a hypothesis and apply a fix. Wrong fixes hurt. Pick the right one and the incident ends.

Things to notice: is the decoy tempting? Does "mitigate first" feel worth its Focus? Is the penalty for guessing fair? Would you want to play another one? Not sure where to start? Jump to the guided walkthroughs at the bottom, or just poke at the system map.
